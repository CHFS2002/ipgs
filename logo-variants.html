IPGS — Logo Design Variants

International Plant Grafting Society · 4 concepts for review

Variant 1 — "The Graft Fork"
Botanical Y-fork with a gold graft-tape band across the junction. The most science-specific mark — directly illustrates the rootstock–scion union. Reads cleanly at all sizes.
IPGS International Plant Grafting Society

✦ Most legible at 44 px. Gold band is the visual anchor. Suitable as website favicon, app icon, and standalone mark.

Variant 2 — "Society Seal"
Traditional circular academic emblem with full society name on the perimeter ring. Authoritative and institutional — suited for formal documents, certificates, letterheads, and stamps.
IPGS International Plant Grafting Society

✦ Traditional seal format used by ISHS, ASHS, and other horticultural societies. Text ring may blur below 64 px — use simplified circle variant as favicon.

Variant 3 — "Dual Leaf Union"
Two opposing leaves with stems converging at a gold graft junction point. Represents the rootstock–scion meeting. Abstract yet botanical — modern, versatile, gender-neutral scientific aesthetic.
IPGS International Plant Grafting Society

✦ Wide aspect ratio suits horizontal header bars. The two-tone green leaves (darker = rootstock, lighter = scion) carry symbolic meaning. Gold node is the icon-scale anchor.

Variant 4 — "Globe Graft"
An Earth globe with a graft-fork plant silhouette on the face. Immediately signals international scope. Combines scientific and geographic symbolism — distinctive and memorable.
IPGS International Plant Grafting Society

✦ Globe motif reinforces "International" at a glance. Works especially well on dark backgrounds and digital banners. Boldest choice — most distinctive at distance.
